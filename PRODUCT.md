# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Malaysian and Southeast Asian business owners, brand managers and Web3 founders, often arriving from LinkedIn. They are deciding whether to hire Rene Fischer-Bernard as a technology consultant (blockchain, decentralised AI, innovation strategy), or Inspirilab to build software.

## Product Purpose
Inspirilab Sdn. Bhd. (Cyberjaya) is an IT consultation and solution design company. The site's job is to get qualified visitors to book Rene for consulting; secondary, to show the build services (SaaS & AI products, websites & migrations, blockchain & DeAI). Success = consultation enquiries.

## Positioning
A hands-on consultant whose own team also ships the product: advice from someone with 15+ years in blockchain and energy, backed by real built work (LuxTag/BrandTag, PakcikLocation, Morpheus DeAI). Not advice-only, not build-only.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Nuxt 3 + Tailwind v4 (@nuxt/ui v3), static generate. All content lives in `data/site.ts`.
- No backend: contact is a mailto form (shown only once a company email is set) or LinkedIn.
- Undecided / pending from Rene: company enquiry email, Rene's photo, Storesini/SafeGate details, client logos and testimonials (ask Rene; never fabricate).

## Brand Commitments
- Name "Inspirilab", existing wordmark `public/logo.png` (light wordmark made for dark backgrounds), brand green #23b573.
- Partners: MOR.org and Morpheus.asia.

## Evidence on Hand
- Project sites and links: LuxTag, BrandTag, PakcikLocation GBP Audit (auditgbp.my), The Jungle Coffee, MOR.org, Morpheus.asia.
- Rene's credentials and talks (from his LinkedIn): co-founded LuxTag 2017, Dipl.-Ing. Industrial Engineering (Hochschule Zittau/Görlitz), HarvardX certificate, keynote World Blockchain Summit KL 2019, Symbol Hackathon mentor 2021, SuperDeAI 2025, Moscow 2018.
- Absent, must not be invented: client logos, testimonials, metrics, prices.

## Product Principles
- Consulting first; build services support it.
- Prove with real work and real credentials, never claims.
- Honest, no-frills tone (Rene offers "no-frills honest reviews").
